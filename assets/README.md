# Gothic game assets

47 images generated with Grok Imagine through Computer Use.

Organized into player, weapons, enemies, bosses, pets, tiles, props, items, and screens. See `preview.jpg` for a contact sheet and `manifest.json` for dimensions and filenames.

Square images are 1408×1408; widescreen sheets and screens are 1792×1008. Original JPEG exports retain their magenta backgrounds; the cultist uses green. Transparency, individual sheet slicing, animation rigging, and tile seam validation have not been applied.

Some clipped prompt phrases were repaired from context. The two missing ability entries were filled with spectral dash and raven swarm; the missing armor entry was filled with a closed plate helm.
